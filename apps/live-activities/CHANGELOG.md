# Live Activities

## 1.0.1

- Tinted activities keep their tint's saturation, so dark tints like Termius' stay dark instead of turning bright blue.
- Buttons and images no longer show up dimmed, as if pressed.
- Text wraps, truncates, aligns and spaces like it does on the phone, using the app's own fonts where it ships them.
- Frosted materials, inner shadows and angular gradients draw properly.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Shows your iPhone's Live Activities on the Car Thing, mirrored from your Mac through the bridgething desktop app.
- Redraws each activity's lock-screen view natively from the data macOS receives, at the Car Thing's resolution.
- Ticks timers and progress bars on the device, and rolls changed digits like iOS.
- Comes to the front when an activity starts, or also when one updates, or never (setting), and returns to the
  previous app when the last one ends.
